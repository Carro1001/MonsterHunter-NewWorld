# MonsterHunter-NewWorld
A Monster Hunter themed mod for Minecraft

Requires [TerraBlender](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/terrablender) 4.1.0.8 for
NeoForge 1.21.1, on both the client and the server. Note that the Verdant Hunting Grounds biome only
appears in newly generated terrain, so an existing world has to be explored outward to find it.

## The first hunting loop

Find the Verdant Hunting Grounds, hunt what lives there, and make something out of it:

1. **Hunt.** Great Izuchi travel with a pack of small Izuchi escorts. The escorts circle at a
   distance and take turns darting in rather than all piling on at once.
2. **Carve.** Sneak and right-click a fresh Great Izuchi, small Izuchi or Aptonoth body with
   your main hand. You get **three carves of your own** if you actually helped bring it down --
   your own hits, your arrows, or your tamed pet's. Everybody who fought gets their own three; one
   hunter's carves never eat another's. The body stays carvable for 10 real minutes of loaded time
   and pauses while the chunk is unloaded or the server is down. These three species drop nothing on
   death: carving is the reward.
3. **Cook.** Raw meat cooks in a furnace, smoker or campfire, exactly like beef.
4. **Craft.** Hide, claws and bones make the four bone-armor pieces. A full set costs 5 hide,
   4 claws and 15 bones -- about two fully carved Great Izuchi plus some ordinary skeleton bones.
5. **Wear it.** Bone armor protects like iron, and wearing all four pieces at once adds
   +0.1 knockback resistance. Take one piece off and the bonus goes with it.

## The Giant Jawblade

A bone greatsword: four bones, two claws, two hides and a stick. It hits for 9 and swings slowly
(0.8 a second), and it is repaired with bone rather than iron.

Hold right-click for a second and a half and it makes one committed strike that reaches 4.5 blocks
-- further than a normal swing, straight down your crosshair, one target only. A wall stops it. Let
go early and nothing happens at all. Whether it hits or misses, the weapon needs a second and a half
to recover, so it is reach bought with commitment, not a better attack. Ordinary left-click is still
the faster option up close.

*Its look is temporary:* it currently borrows the iron sword's icon while the real blade art is
finished.

## Field preparation

Three things the wildlife is good for besides materials:

- **BBQ spit.** Raw meat plus a stick. Hold right-click for four seconds anywhere and it becomes
  cooked meat -- no furnace, no fuel, just the time.
- **Flash bombs.** Right-click a live Flashbug with a glass bottle to catch it, then combine the
  bottle with paper for one flash bomb (you get the bottle back). Throw it and anything nearby that
  is looking at it goes blind and slow for two seconds. It never hurts you, and never hurts players
  at all -- look away, get behind something, or back off and it misses you.
- **Toads in buckets.** Catch any of the four toad variants with a water bucket and carry it. Put it
  down where you want it and it sits there, same variant, until something hits it -- then it does
  what it always did. If you set off a blast toad yourself, the damage it deals counts as yours for
  carving.

# MonsterHunter-NewWorld
A Monster Hunter themed mod for Minecraft

Requires [TerraBlender](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/terrablender) 4.1.0.8 for
NeoForge 1.21.1, on both the client and the server. Note that the Verdant Hunting Grounds biome only
appears in newly generated terrain, so an existing world has to be explored outward to find it.

## The first hunting loop

Find the Verdant Hunting Grounds, hunt what lives there, and make something out of it:

1. **Hunt.** Great Izuchi travel with a pack of small Izuchi escorts. The escorts circle at a
   distance and take turns darting in rather than all piling on at once.
2. **Carve.** Sneak and right-click a fresh Great Izuchi, small Izuchi or Aptonoth body with
   your main hand. You get **three carves of your own** if you actually helped bring it down --
   your own hits, your arrows, or your tamed pet's. Everybody who fought gets their own three; one
   hunter's carves never eat another's. The body stays carvable for 10 real minutes of loaded time
   and pauses while the chunk is unloaded or the server is down. These three species drop nothing on
   death: carving is the reward.
3. **Cook.** Raw meat cooks in a furnace, smoker or campfire, exactly like beef.
4. **Craft.** Hide, claws and bones make the four bone-armor pieces. A full set costs 5 hide,
   4 claws and 15 bones -- about two fully carved Great Izuchi plus some ordinary skeleton bones.
5. **Wear it.** Bone armor protects like iron, and wearing all four pieces at once adds
   +0.1 knockback resistance. Take one piece off and the bonus goes with it.
